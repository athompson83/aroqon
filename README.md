# Aroqon HQ

HQ is the Product Owner's business dashboard, and the Co-Founder agent keeps
it current:

- **Overview** shows the Co-Founder's latest brief and your open to-dos,
  including P0 and overdue items. It also shows whether each agent is on
  track, the mail that needs you, delivery problems, analytics trends, project
  health, and each agent's last activity.
- **To-do** is your one list. The Co-Founder adds to it, and you add, edit,
  reprioritise and close items.
- **Mail** shows the mail received and sent through Resend, organised into
  Inbox, Needs attention, Sent and Handled, and by category. You can reply,
  mark mail handled or re-file it. The mail client is adapted from
  [shadcn/ui's Mail example](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui) (MIT).

`docs/COFOUNDER.md` explains how it all fits together. `AGENTS.md` has the
rules for changing it.
